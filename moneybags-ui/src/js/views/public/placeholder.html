MoneyBag principle
01

Identity sign-in

Customer and bank-admin sessions use separate OIDC clients and only the two supported roles.

02

Server-side tokens

The BFF holds OAuth tokens, applies CSRF protection, and injects trusted tenant and correlation headers.

03

KYC-gated access

Customers can register and complete onboarding, but banking modules remain locked until KYC is approved.

01

Browse openly

Anyone can compare the active deposit and credit-card catalogue before creating a sign-in.

02

Bank modularly

Accounts, fixed deposits, cards, payments, bills, KYC, Product Master, Accounting, and IAM have focused pages.

03

State boundaries honestly

Statement generation and full EOD orchestration remain visible but are not represented as completed services.
